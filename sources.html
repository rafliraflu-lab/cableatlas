How we research

Technical sources & further reading

The standards bodies and manufacturers determine what a product supports. We translate their concepts into practical questions, without claiming independent device testing or certification.

Standards organizations and primary documentation

  • USB-IF — Cables and Connectors: USB-C cable compliance information, power markings and data capability labels.
  • USB-IF — USB Power Delivery: power negotiation, supported charging levels and direction.
  • VESA — DisplayPort FAQ: DisplayPort over USB-C, adapter direction and host support.
  • HDMI Licensing Administrator — Cable Overview: cable categories and certification guidance.
  • IEEE 802.3 working group: published Ethernet standards and project documents.

How to verify an individual product

Find the official support page for the exact device model. Check source-port capability, cable or adapter specifications, receiving-device input, supported operating systems, and limitations when devices are connected simultaneously. If documentation is vague, contact the manufacturer before paying for an accessory.

What these links do not prove

A standards organization’s general description does not certify an untested product or make a universal claim about every USB-C port. CableAtlas does not operate a test lab, has not conducted hands-on comparisons of named products, and does not endorse brands.
