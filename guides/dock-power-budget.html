Reading a dock power budget

Distinguish dock input power from host output.

Updated 18 November 2026 · By Saiful Bahri · Device-specific verification required

Worked example

A dock may have a 100 W supply but advertise only 65 W host charging. If the laptop documentation requests 90 W, the host-output shortfall is 25 W; the total supply label cannot resolve it. A second display can also change bandwidth requirements.

Repeatable check

Read host output rather than total dock input. List occupied USB ports and display modes, then confirm the manufacturer’s simultaneous-use table for the exact host OS. Test charging and one monitor separately before adding peripherals.

Interpret the result

This planner cannot model internal power allocation or prove that a laptop accepts a profile. Verify actual negotiated charging through supported system diagnostics.

Illustrative calculation or scenario, not an independent hardware test. Keep your own device records to verify the setup.
