Why charging time is not battery capacity divided by charger watts

Work with watt-hours and average power.

Updated 18 November 2026 · By Saiful Bahri · Device-specific verification required

Worked example

A 60 Wh battery going from 20% to 80% gains roughly 36 Wh. If average input is 45 W and 85% reaches the battery, the simple model gives 0.94 hours. At 20 W average input it gives 2.12 hours.

Repeatable check

Record starting charge, ending charge, elapsed time and whether the laptop was in use. Compare two sessions only under similar workload and temperature. Use the manufacturer battery health information; rated and usable capacity may differ.

Interpret the result

Power taper and concurrent device use make charger nameplate power a poor substitute for measured average power. Do not bypass charge protection.

Illustrative calculation or scenario, not an independent hardware test. Keep your own device records to verify the setup.
