TRS, TRRS and optical audio connections

Distinguish physical audio connectors from wiring and signal format.

Updated 18 November 2026 · By Saiful Bahri · Device-specific verification required

Worked example

A three-contact 3.5 mm TRS headset plug can fit a four-contact socket, but that does not guarantee microphone support. An optical TOSLINK input accepts digital audio, not analog headphone voltage. An ordinary passive mechanical adapter cannot translate these signal types.

Repeatable check

Identify the source output type and destination input type. For headsets consult the documented jack wiring convention. For optical connections check supported audio format and input selection. Test known-good equipment at low volume.

Interpret the result

Do not insert oversized connectors or assume headphone and line-level ports are interchangeable. Use a documented converter when changing signal type.

Illustrative calculation or scenario, not an independent hardware test. Keep your own device records to verify the setup.
