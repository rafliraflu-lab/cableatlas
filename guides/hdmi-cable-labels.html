HDMI cable certification labels and link rates

Read the cable category alongside endpoint capabilities.

Updated 18 November 2026 · By Saiful Bahri · Device-specific verification required

Worked example

Ultra High Speed HDMI cables support system configurations up to 48 Gbit/s. Ultra96 cables address configurations up to 96 Gbit/s under HDMI 2.2. A device implementing HDMI 2.2 does not necessarily enable its maximum link rate or every optional feature.

Repeatable check

Check the monitor input table for each advertised mode. Find the source maximum link capability and actual cable category. Verify certification information where available and test a known-good short direct cable before changing equipment.

Interpret the result

Cable category alone cannot guarantee a resolution or refresh rate. Avoid purchasing based solely on a version number printed in a marketplace listing.

Illustrative calculation or scenario, not an independent hardware test. Keep your own device records to verify the setup.
