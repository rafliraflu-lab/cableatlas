Why Some Adapters Work Only One Way

Learn why a USB-C-to-HDMI or HDMI-to-DisplayPort adapter is not always reversible.

Reviewed November 4, 2026 · CableAtlas Editorial Desk · Approximately 4-minute read

Key principle: A connection works only when the devices, ports, cables, and adapters support the required feature together. Always verify your specific device model.

Signal conversion is directional

A connector adapter may contain active electronics designed to convert a signal from a specific source to a specific display input.

Read source and destination labels

An adapter described as “DisplayPort source to HDMI display” should not be assumed to work with an HDMI source and DisplayPort monitor.

Power may be required

Some active converters require USB power. A missing power connection can make an otherwise correct setup fail.

Confirm the target mode

Check supported resolution, refresh rate, audio, HDR and other features, rather than relying on “4K” marketing alone.

Before purchase

Write down the source device port, destination device port, and desired mode. Search for an adapter explicitly supporting that direction.

Verification checklist

  1. Find the exact model numbers of both devices.
  2. Read the official manufacturer specification for each port.
  3. Check the cable or adapter rating and required direction.
  4. Test a direct connection before adding hubs or docks.
  5. Record what worked so you can repeat the setup.
