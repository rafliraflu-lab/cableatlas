Adapter chains and one-way conversions

Draw signal direction before choosing a converter.

Updated 18 November 2026 · By Saiful Bahri · Device-specific verification required

Worked example

An HDMI output on a computer feeding a DisplayPort input needs an adapter specified for HDMI source to DP display. A DP-source-to-HDMI-display product is a different path. A USB-C output must support the intended video protocol before an ordinary USB-C video adapter can work.

Repeatable check

Draw source → converter input → converter output → display. Copy the exact words from each specification. Check whether the converter needs external power, and test the source and display directly where possible.

Interpret the result

Connector fittings alone do not establish signal conversion. Do not join two adapters based on plug gender without verifying protocols and direction.

Illustrative calculation or scenario, not an independent hardware test. Keep your own device records to verify the setup.
