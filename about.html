About & Editorial Policy

CableAtlas is an independent educational reference about common cables, ports, and connection problems. We are not affiliated with USB-IF, HDMI Licensing Administrator, Intel, or device manufacturers.

Our editorial approach

We write for specific reader questions: what a connector can do, why a connection fails, and what to verify before buying. Articles distinguish connector shape from supported capabilities and avoid promising compatibility without device-specific evidence.

How we research

We prioritize official standards organizations and manufacturer documentation, including USB-IF, HDMI, VESA DisplayPort, and manufacturer support pages. These links are background references, not endorsements or claims of certification.

Updates and corrections

Technology standards evolve. Guides are reviewed for clarity and updated when material errors are found. Last editorial review: November 4, 2026. Send corrections with a source link and the affected page to rafliraflu@gmail.com.

Transparency

We may display advertisements through Google AdSense. Advertising does not determine our technical recommendations. We do not currently sell cables or rank products for commissions.

How readers can check our information

For each cable decision, distinguish the connector visible on the outside from the signal, power, and data features supported by the exact device model. Product claims can be incomplete; do not treat a marketing photo as a specification. We link to standards organizations and ask readers to verify the manufacturer documentation relevant to their device and region.

We provide general educational explanations, not independent laboratory certification. Worked examples describe hypothetical troubleshooting situations; they are not reports of product testing. A correction request should include the page URL, what appears wrong, and an authoritative reference that supports the proposed change.
