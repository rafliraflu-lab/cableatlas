Method and limits

Power = voltage × current. Only use values from a common supported profile; a 5 A cable does not establish EPR support. Above 20 V requires compatible EPR equipment and cable. Never force voltage or change wiring.

Reference: https://www.usb.org/usb-charger-pd. Check exact device documentation for implementation details.
