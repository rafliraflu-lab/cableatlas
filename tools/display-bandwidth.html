Method and limits

For 3840 × 2160 at 60 Hz and 10-bit RGB, the active image needs 14.92992 Gbit/s. Blanking, transport overhead, audio, compression, and lane allocation are excluded. Compare with payload, not raw link rate. A result below a ceiling is necessary, not sufficient.
