Connection audit worksheet

Turn “my USB-C cable does not work” into a description a support technician or seller can act on. Save the device capability, exact symptom and next experiment. No account, uploads or third-party tracking are needed for this worksheet.

Why the worksheet helps

It separates facts verified from an official product specification from assumptions based on connector appearance. A controlled test changes only one component. For instance, a monitor that works with the laptop but not through a dock suggests checking dock compatibility and host bandwidth before replacing the monitor.

Never share private device serial numbers or passwords in a support forum. This page stores nothing on the server; exporting is an optional local download.
