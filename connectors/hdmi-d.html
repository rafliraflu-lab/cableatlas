Micro HDMI (Type D)

Very small HDMI shell.

Typical use

Compact cameras and embedded boards.

What the form does not prove

Can be confused with Micro-USB; do not insert by force.

Verification step

Identify the socket from the device manual.

Reference: https://www.hdmi.org/spec/hdmi2. Check exact device documentation for implementation details.
