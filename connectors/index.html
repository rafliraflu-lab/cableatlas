CableAtlas · Connectivity reference

Connector Atlas

Twenty illustrated connector references. Use physical identification together with model-specific capabilities.

Not a pinout or measured product catalog. Similar shells and even identical connectors can support different protocols.

USB-C

USB data, negotiated charging, optional display and USB4.

USB Type-A

USB peripherals, data and limited power.

USB Type-B

Printers, audio interfaces and older desktop peripherals.

Micro-USB Type-B

Older phones and accessories, typically USB 2.0.

Mini-USB Type-B

Older cameras, GPS and instruments.

SuperSpeed Micro-B

Older external hard drives using SuperSpeed USB.

HDMI Type A

Digital video and audio for TVs and monitors.

Mini HDMI (Type C)

Digital video from some cameras and tablets.

Micro HDMI (Type D)

Compact cameras and embedded boards.

DisplayPort

Digital display and audio connections.

Mini DisplayPort

Legacy laptop and display video connections.

8P8C Ethernet (RJ45-style)

Twisted-pair Ethernet networking.

VGA (DE-15)

Analog RGB video on legacy displays.

DVI-D dual link

Legacy digital video.

DVI-I dual link

Legacy digital and analog display output when supported.

Lightning

Compatible older Apple mobile accessories.

3.5 mm TRS

Typically stereo analog audio or balanced mono, depending on equipment.

3.5 mm TRRS

Common headset audio and microphone connection.

TOSLINK optical

Digital optical audio.

LC fiber connector

Optical networking via compatible transceivers.
