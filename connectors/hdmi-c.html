Mini HDMI (Type C)

Smaller trapezoidal HDMI shell.

Typical use

Digital video from some cameras and tablets.

What the form does not prove

Mini HDMI is different from Micro HDMI; no higher bandwidth is implied by size.

Verification step

Use the correct physical adapter and check camera output mode.

Reference: https://www.hdmi.org/spec/hdmi2. Check exact device documentation for implementation details.
