DVI-D dual link

Wide rectangular contact grid plus flat blade.

Typical use

Legacy digital video.

What the form does not prove

DVI-D has no analog VGA signal; dual-link needs compatible source and sink.

Verification step

Confirm single/dual-link requirements before choosing an adapter.
