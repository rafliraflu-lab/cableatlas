LC fiber connector

Small keyed fiber plug, often paired for duplex links.

Typical use

Optical networking via compatible transceivers.

What the form does not prove

Fiber type, wavelength and polish must match; never look into a live fiber.

Verification step

Use the transceiver data sheet and qualified installer for cleaning and inspection.

Reference: https://www.foa.org/. Check exact device documentation for implementation details.
