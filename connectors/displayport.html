DisplayPort

Rectangular shell with one clipped corner.

Typical use

Digital display and audio connections.

What the form does not prove

Lane rate, count and supported modes vary; some plugs latch.

Verification step

Release the latch where present before unplugging.

Reference: https://www.displayport.org/faq/. Check exact device documentation for implementation details.
