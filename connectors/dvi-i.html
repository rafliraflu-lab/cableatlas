DVI-I dual link

Contact grid with four analog contacts around a blade.

Typical use

Legacy digital and analog display output when supported.

What the form does not prove

Analog contacts do not mean every device activates the analog signal.

Verification step

Read source manual before using a passive VGA adapter.

Reference: https://www.hdmi.org/. Check exact device documentation for implementation details.
