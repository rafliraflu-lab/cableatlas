TOSLINK optical

Keyed square-ish optical connector.

Typical use

Digital optical audio.

What the form does not prove

Does not carry analog audio; supported formats depend on equipment.

Verification step

Remove dust caps and choose optical input on the receiver.

Reference: https://www.iec.ch/. Check exact device documentation for implementation details.
